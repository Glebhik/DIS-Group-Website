About DIS Group

Built on Compliance,
Driven by Value.

DIS Group — Dismelt Irish Services Group — is a County Wicklow-based industrial services company specialising in the responsible processing of end-of-life materials.

Our Story

Ireland's Circular
Economy Specialist

DIS Group operates at the intersection of waste management, metal trading, and environmental compliance. We were built on a straightforward principle: every tonne of scrap metal, every end-of-life vehicle, and every obsolete industrial asset represents a resource — not a problem.

Based in County Wicklow and serving Leinster and beyond, we extract that value legally, efficiently, and sustainably — whether that means grading aluminium for a European smelter, processing a fleet of end-of-life vehicles, or coordinating a full industrial site clearance.

  • EPA licensed facility operating under full regulatory oversight
  • Authorised Treatment Facility (ATF) for end-of-life vehicle processing
  • Waste transfer notes and Certificates of Destruction issued as standard
  • Weighbridge-certified weights on all commercial material trades
  • Fully insured, compliant with ELV Directive and Irish Waste Regulations

Our Values

How We Operate

Three principles guide every job, every collection, and every trade we conduct.

🌿
Environmental Responsibility

EPA-permitted operations with zero tolerance for illegal disposal. Full waste transfer documentation on every load. Sustainability is not a marketing position at DIS Group — it is the operating model.

⚙️
Operational Efficiency

We run our own plant, machinery, and logistics. That keeps costs controlled and turnaround fast. When you book a collection or a site clearance, you deal with the team that actually does the work.

📋
Regulatory Compliance

Every regulatory requirement — from waste transfer notes to Certificates of Destruction to EPA reporting — is handled in-house as a matter of course. Compliance is built into the process, not added at the end.
