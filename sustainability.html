Our Approach

Environment,
Economy, Community

Our operating model is grounded in three pillars that reinforce each other. Environmental compliance creates commercial trust. Commercial efficiency funds continued investment. Community responsibility ensures we remain a responsible presence in Wicklow.

Environment

EPA-permitted operations with zero tolerance for illegal disposal. All waste streams are classified, documented, and processed through licensed facilities. Contaminated material is segregated and disposed of in accordance with hazardous waste regulations.

Economy

Keeping secondary materials in the Irish and European economy — supplying certified aluminium feedstock to downstream manufacturers and reducing dependence on virgin extraction. Sustainable processing creates durable commercial value.

Community

Wicklow-based and locally operated. Clean site, proper operating hours, and responsible neighbour conduct. We are building a long-term facility — not a short-term operation — and that shapes every decision we make.

A note on aluminium: Secondary aluminium requires only 5% of the energy of primary production. For every tonne of aluminium we recycle instead of mining, we eliminate approximately 8–9 tonnes of CO₂ equivalent from the production chain. At our current processing volumes, that is a material contribution to Ireland's industrial decarbonisation targets.
