Our Services

Service 01

Aluminium Recycling

High-volume collection, grading, and processing of post-consumer and post-industrial aluminium scrap. We buy directly from manufacturers, demolition companies, and trade customers, and supply certified secondary aluminium to smelters across Europe.

Recycling aluminium uses 95% less energy than primary smelting. Every tonne we process displaces virgin extraction, reduces landfill, and cuts CO₂ at source.

Service 02

Vehicle Dismantling

Licensed end-of-life vehicle (ELV) processing under the EU ELV Directive and Irish ELV Regulations 2006. As an Authorised Treatment Facility (ATF), we carry out full depollution, parts recovery, and issue Certificates of Destruction on every vehicle we process.

The Certificate of Destruction is a legal requirement for removing a vehicle from the national vehicle register. We issue it correctly and on time — every time.

📄
Certificate of Destruction (CoD) Legally required proof of end-of-life disposal. Issued electronically to the vehicle owner and the national register.
🛢️
Full Depollution Fuel, oil, coolant, brake fluid, and refrigerants removed and disposed of in accordance with EPA requirements.
🔧
Parts Recovery Usable components catalogued and recovered prior to shredding — maximising reuse and reducing waste-to-landfill.
