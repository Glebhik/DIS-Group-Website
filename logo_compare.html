Why V2 — Circular Badge — was selected for the homepage

Four criteria evaluated: visual impact, element legibility, format versatility, and brand communication.

Globe is the dominant canvas

In V2 the blue globe fills the entire badge circle as a background, making DIS Group's global/European trading reach the first thing a visitor sees — not a small icon competing for space.

Recycling arrows read at hero scale

The D arrows in V2 are drawn at stroke-width 12, three times thicker than V1's badge version. At the 300px display size in the hero section they are unmistakably recycling arrows forming a D.

Circular format suits a corporate hero section

A wide hero with text on the left and a circular emblem on the right is a proven corporate web layout (think Stripe, Salesforce, Bloomberg). V1 is too horizontal; V3's hexagon is too "badge-like" at large scale.

Wrench and ring text communicate more at scale

V2 includes circular ring text that reads "Dismelt Irish Services Group · Ireland" — impossible in the small badge of V1. At hero size this adds credibility without needing a separate tagline line.

Where each logo is used on the homepage
V1 — Navbar & Footer Horizontal format fits perfectly in the 50px-tall navbar strip and the footer brand column.
V2 — Hero Section ★ Displayed at ~300px on the right column of the hero — the first large visual a visitor sees.
V3 — About Section The hexagonal stacked version sits inside the About card, reinforcing the industrial brand identity.
